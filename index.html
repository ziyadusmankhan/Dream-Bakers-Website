A few things you might be wondering.

Can I share my own cake design?

Yes. Choose “My own custom design” in the consultation form. You can attach your inspiration image directly in WhatsApp and discuss what is possible with the baker.

How much will my cake cost?

Cake varieties have per-pound base prices on our menu. Design, customization and delivery charges may apply; the bakery confirms the final quotation and availability on WhatsApp.

Can I arrange pickup or delivery?

Select pickup or home delivery in your request. The bakery will confirm arrangements, delivery coverage and any charges on WhatsApp.

Is my cake confirmed when I send the form?

The form prepares a quotation request. Send it in WhatsApp, then confirm the final details and availability directly with Dream Bakerz.
